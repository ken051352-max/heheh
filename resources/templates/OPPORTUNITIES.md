Copy this into a private planning folder. It supports the [career guide](../../guides/career-operations.md#skills-checklist) and [semester review](../../guides/india-undergraduate-path.md#semester-review). Reopen official pages before applying. Do not store identity documents, account credentials or confidential interview material here.

## Evidence from the posting

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Keep short exact quotations for consequential eligibility conditions. Record the types of experience accepted: employment, internship, research, coursework or personal projects. A title such as “junior” does not settle this. Write “unstated” rather than inferring sponsorship or permission to work remotely from India.

For internships, add duration, weekly hours, compensation and its stated period, travel and return-to-study conditions. Check compatibility with your semester. If a page is expired, closed, inaccessible or contradictory, record that and do not treat it as an open vacancy.
