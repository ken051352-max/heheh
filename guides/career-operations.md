# Career operations

Build relevant competence and make it easy for a reviewer to assess. Hiring also depends on vacancies, geography, work authorization, credentials, timing and individual circumstances. These planning suggestions are not measured guarantees about hiring.

**First-year CSE/AI in India, role undecided?** Use the [undergraduate international-options route](india-undergraduate-path.md). It keeps first-year foundations broad, introduces small role experiments and reviews specialization each semester. The [November 2026 employer sample](../audit/career-research-2026-11-05.md) records 12 postings and student-program guidance; it is evidence about particular roles, not a forecast of your graduation-year market.

## 1. Choose an attainable next role

Use the [role pathways](practitioner-track.md#role-pathways) to define a target, then compare it with current postings at the location and seniority you can realistically pursue. Learn the work behind tool names: data validation, evaluation, deployment and communication transfer across implementations.

## 2. Apply with evidence

Distinguish mandatory eligibility from preferred qualifications. Apply when you can demonstrate the central work and meet the actual eligibility conditions; no fixed percentage-match rule applies. A credential or authorization requirement cannot be waived by completing this roadmap.

Keep a private application log: role, employer, URL, date, location, seniority, required evidence, stage and next action. Review patterns periodically. A rejection is incomplete feedback; do not diagnose a resume or ability problem from a small conversion count alone.

Use the [opportunity template](../resources/templates/OPPORTUNITIES.md) to separate **required, preferred and unstated** conditions. Record degree/discipline, graduation window, accepted experience types, work country, remote-location restrictions, sponsorship and availability. Do not interpret a “junior” title, company headquarters or a general sponsorship FAQ as proof of eligibility. Check internship hours and dates against college commitments.

## 3. Learn from interviews

Prepare examples of a technical decision, failure, investigation and collaboration. Ask what the team builds and how it evaluates success. After an interview, record the questions, what you could demonstrate and what needs practice. Map actionable gaps to a module or project rather than restarting the whole curriculum.

<a id="interview-practice"></a>
### Rehearse the work of the chosen role

These are curriculum exercises derived from job tasks, not claims about how often employers ask particular questions. Rehearse one relevant exercise, ask a peer to change the inputs, explain the trade-off and record the gap. Check the actual interview format when invited; advanced system-design expectations vary with seniority.

| Role | Practice exercise | Evidence to explain |
|---|---|---|
| Analyst | Write a join/window query, reconcile a workbook total, diagnose a misleading chart | Grain, duplicate handling, denominator, uncertainty and the decision supported |
| Data scientist | Diagnose leakage, choose a metric/threshold and compare a baseline; outline an experiment | Split design, assumptions, error slices, uncertainty and limits of causal claims |
| Data engineer | Debug a replay/schema-change failure in an incremental pipeline | Keys, data contracts, late data, recovery and tests that reproduce the bug |
| ML engineer | Diagnose train/serve preprocessing mismatch and an operational regression | Model/data versions, quality gate, resource trade-offs, monitoring and rollback |
| AI application engineer | Trace a failed tool workflow and demonstrate schema/permission/idempotency checks | Deterministic baseline, model-quality evidence, bounded retries and safe failure |
| Software / research direction | Software: solve and test a changed coding problem. Research: explain and critique a reproduced result | Software: complexity, design and debugging. Research: assumptions, baselines, ablations and uncertainty |

Prepare a short walkthrough and a deeper technical explanation of your project. State what you did, how you tested it and what remains weak. Label personal projects, coursework, simulations and employment accurately; inventing users, revenue or production scale undermines the evidence. [Atlassian's engineering handbook](https://www.atlassian.com/company/careers/resources/interviewing/engineering) is one inspected employer example covering code, design and collaboration; it is not every company's or every internship's process.

## 4. Outreach and community

Send a short, relevant message when you have a concrete question or useful contribution. Respect the recipient's time and boundaries; avoid mass outreach quotas. Peer review, mentoring and technical communities can provide feedback. A private study log and a regular peer check-in are valid alternatives to posting publicly.

<a id="5-build-for-real-people-not-for-datasets"></a>
## 5. Build work that someone can inspect

A real stakeholder can clarify constraints, but a carefully designed public-data project or benchmark reproduction can also show strong skills. You count as a user. State the problem, baseline, data rights, metric, failure cases and decision the result supports.

| Artifact | What a reviewer can verify |
|---|---|
| README | Problem, scope, environment and one command to reproduce the main result |
| Tests and evaluation | Correctness, leakage controls, baseline comparison, uncertainty and important failures |
| Results memo | What changed, how measured, limitations and the next decision |
| Design record | Alternatives, evidence, trade-offs and a rollback/revision condition |
| Demo | A sanitized example or local reproduction; public hosting is optional |

Competition work can demonstrate modeling and evaluation when rules permit sharing. Add your own error analysis and methodological explanation; do not assume a medal or a named stakeholder is necessary for every role.

## 6. Accountability

Choose a sustainable cadence for reviewing progress. Record what you built and your next action. Request feedback on a specific artifact. Public visibility may help others discover your work, but course policies, employer agreements and privacy requirements determine what can be shared.

## 7. Timelines and opportunity costs

Estimate study time from your actual pace over several weeks. The [FAQ](../FAQ.md#how-long) and [four-year plan](four-year-plan.md) use planning assumptions, not employment deadlines. Pause optional depth when another requirement matters more; preserve a short resume note so returning is easy.

<a id="skills-checklist"></a>
## 8. Build your own role evidence sample

Review 10–15 current postings as a **small exploratory sample**, chosen for your target geography, seniority and industry. Record the selection criteria and retrieval date; deduplicate agencies and reposts. Distinguish required from preferred skills and explicit mentions from your interpretation. A sample can guide your next project; it does not estimate the whole labor market.

For an undecided undergraduate, review two plausible role families each semester and again before the relevant recruitment season. Include roles you can become eligible for, rather than pooling experienced US roles with India internships. Keep core CS/math knowledge stable; change one project or implementation choice when evidence justifies it. Record current program deadlines and eligibility from official pages instead of extending this year's dates into future years.

| Target work | Candidate module evidence |
|---|---|
| Analyst/product analysis | M7 data validation and plots; M8a SQL; M6 uncertainty; M6½ experimental design; M25 memo |
| Applied data science | Above plus M9–M12 leakage-safe predictive evaluation; M13/M14 when the role requires them |
| ML engineering | Packaging, model/data versioning, model evaluation, serving and rollback through M24 |
| AI applications | M18-app typed boundary; M21 retrieval evaluation; M22 tool control; M23 threat tests; M24 operations |
| Data engineering | M8a schema/query work; M8b replay, transformations and orchestration; M24 operational evidence |
| Research | Relevant theory, paper reproduction, ablations, uncertainty and the M26 research report |

Earlier posting samples remain in the [historical audit](../audit/AUDIT_v2026.3.md). Their mention counts and job titles are dated observations, not current prevalence or entry-level requirements. The [WEF Future of Jobs report](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/) is an employer survey; [BLS projections](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm) describe a US occupation. Neither substitutes for checking your target roles.

## A weekly routine

After your initial Python work, allocate a small recurring slot to update one artifact, inspect relevant opportunities and practice explaining a decision. Increase interview preparation when an actual process approaches. Keep the curriculum, project and career activities within your total available hours.
