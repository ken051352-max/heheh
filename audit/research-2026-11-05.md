## Method and evidence boundaries

The orchestrator and specialist agents inspected author/publisher pages, university course pages, original papers and released technical documentation using live Exa fetch/search and Context7 queries. Major book choices were compared with syllabus use, specialist reviews and dated community discussions. Each source below supports the stated scope; it is not a blanket endorsement of a course, book or vendor.

“Inspected” means returned page/document content or a documentation query was examined. It does not mean every embedded video was played, every book read, every exercise solved or every integration executed. An inaccessible page was not silently treated as available. Shell HTTP/package access was restricted in this environment; live MCP research remained available. This is targeted content research, **not a new full HTTP sweep of every historical URL**.

- Live targeted research corrected meaningful access and metadata defects; it did not refresh every legacy/historical URL or rewatch every practitioner video timestamp.
- Khan's client challenge and ESL's unresolved fetch remain disclosed. Public course availability can change, and a full lecture playlist may contain individually unavailable items.
- The local environment could not install DuckDB from the network. The alternate SQLite route was executed; DuckDB test skips are recorded in the overhaul audit.
- Provider APIs, GPU training, distributed clusters and real MCP transport integrations were not executed. Their course projects specify what a learner must demonstrate beyond the supplied local fixtures.
- The current audit supersedes unqualified “every link verified” statements in historical records; old bodies are preserved as history.
