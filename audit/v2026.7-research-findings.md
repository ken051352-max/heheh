## 3. Level-5 self-improving loops and reward hacking (W3, 08:19–10:18)

- **Karpathy's loop:** [`karpathy/autoresearch`](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) ✅ (March 2026; repo has no licence file and is a single-NVIDIA-GPU research toy). An agent edits only `train.py` (simplified [nanochat](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat)); `prepare.py` (data + evaluation) is never modified; **fixed 5-minute training budget**; metric **val_bpb** (vocab-size-independent); ≈12 experiments/hour; `program.md` = "a super lightweight skill". Karpathy's [post](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2031135152349524125) ⚠️: ~2 days on a depth-12 model found **~20 changes that improved validation loss**. The "~700 experiments" figure appears in secondary reports and in W3, and is consistent with ~12/hour × ~2 days; it is labelled secondary in M23.
- **Reward hacking / specification gaming — the curated three:**
  1. DeepMind, [*Specification gaming: the flip side of AI ingenuity*](https://deepmind.google/blog/specification-gaming-the-flip-side-of-ai-ingenuity/) ✅ (2020) — canonical definition + example list.
  2. METR, [*Recent Frontier Models Are Reward Hacking*](https://metr.org/blog/2025-06-05-recent-reward-hacking/) ✅ (June 2025) — frontier models tampering with scorers and tests during real tasks.
  3. Anthropic, [*Natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking*](https://www.anthropic.com/research/emergent-misalignment-reward-hacking) ✅ — reward hacking in coding environments generalising to broader misbehaviour.
  Also relevant: OpenAI, [*Chain-of-thought monitoring*](https://openai.com/index/chain-of-thought-monitoring/) ⚠️ (bot-gated; not linked from learner pages).
- **Design rules extracted** (freeze the judge, comparable budgets, held-out checks, sandbox, full trails, human approval) → [M23 § Level 5](../coursepages/m23-ai-safety/README.md#level-5-loops).
